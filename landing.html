Sustainability grades for what you buy

Know what you're really buying.

Scan a product, search a brand, or import your receipts. EcoAudit grades it A–F on climate, supply chain, labour, materials and packaging, and calls out green claims that don't hold up.

What it does

Three ways to check something before you buy it.

01

Scan it in the store

Point your phone or laptop camera at a product. EcoAudit identifies it, lets you confirm the match, and returns its grade with the reasons behind it.

02

Search a brand

Type any brand to see its scores in each category, the claims that check out, the ones that don't, and lower-impact alternatives.

03

Import your receipts

Upload receipts or connect Gmail to pull in past purchases. Your dashboard shows your footprint, where things were made, and how far they travelled.

How scores work

Five categories, one grade, and the sources behind it.

Every brand gets a 0–100 score in five categories. The overall score is their average, turned into a letter grade.

A80–100
B58–79
C35–57
D10–34
F0–9

Plus and minus grades split each band.

  • Climate Emissions, targets, and progress against them
  • Supply chain Whether suppliers and factories are disclosed
  • Labour Living wages, forced-labour risk, audits
  • Materials Recycled, organic, and certified inputs
  • Packaging Waste, plastic, and take-back schemes

Hand-checked Brands we've researched

Scores built from B Lab, Good On You, CDP, SBTi, MSCI and the companies' own sustainability reports. Each finding names its source.

AI estimate Everything else

For brands we haven't researched yet, Claude estimates a score from public information. These results are labelled as estimates, so treat them as a starting point, not a verdict.

Greenwashing

Marketing words we don't take at face value.

When a product leans on a claim without evidence behind it, EcoAudit flags it and tells you what's missing.

Eco-friendly

Says nothing measurable. We look for certifications or published data that back it up.

Carbon neutral

Often means offsets, not lower emissions. We check whether emissions are actually falling.

Conscious collection

A small sustainable line can hide the rest of a catalogue. We score the whole brand.

Made with recycled materials

Could be 5% or 95%. We look for the percentage and a certification like GRS or bluesign.
