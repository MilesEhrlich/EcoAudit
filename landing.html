What EcoAudit does

Every feature you need to
shop consciously

From scanning a brand's ESG report to tracking your own footprint and finding more sustainable alternatives.

Camera Product Scanner
Point your camera at any product in-store. AI identifies the item, traces it on screen, and returns an A–F sustainability score with a full breakdown — in seconds.
Greenwash Detection
We flag unverified claims like "eco-friendly", "sustainable cotton", and "carbon neutral" — cross-checked against EU rulings, court cases, and certification databases.
Carbon Footprint Tracker
Uses your location and an estimated carbon footprint from online purchases to show your environmental impact.
ESG Brand Database
Search any brand and see its full scorecard — carbon, labour, materials — graded A–F. Sort by sustainability, filter by category, and see which brands are actually walking the talk.
Browser Extension
Shopping on H&M, Nike, Amazon, Zara? The extension auto-scans the page and shows an eco score badge — before you hit buy.
Impact Dashboard
Track your CO₂ avoided, plastic saved, and money redirected to ethical brands. See your footprint grow or shrink with every scan.
